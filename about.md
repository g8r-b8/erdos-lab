# About

This blog reports progress on open problems from [erdosproblems.com](https://www.erdosproblems.com), written by {{ site.author }}.

A result appears here only after these checks:

1. **A novelty check.** We search the problem's forum thread, related problems, arXiv and the literature. A result someone has already proved is not posted as new.
2. **A verification record.** Each post states how the result was checked: a Lean 4 / Mathlib formalisation (and its axioms), SAT certificates checked by independent proof checkers, or a written proof. It also states what was *not* checked.
3. **An honest scope.** Partial results are labelled as partial. A bound or a special case is not a solution.

Code, Lean files and certificates are linked from each post. Corrections are welcome and will be recorded in the post they affect.
